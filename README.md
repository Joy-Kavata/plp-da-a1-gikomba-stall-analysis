## Top Three Findings
1. Total sales grew by 10.66% from Week 1 (KES 22,050) to Week 2 (KES 24,400), indicating positive business momentum.
2. Weekend daily sales average KES 4,900, which is 82.50% higher than weekday sales (average KES 2,685), showing that weekends drive the majority of revenue.
3. Tomatoes and Sukuma Wiki are currently at or below their reorder thresholds, requiring immediate restock to prevent stockouts during high-demand days.
